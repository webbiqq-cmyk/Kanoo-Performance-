Who We Are

The largest & most advanced automotive customization facility in the Kingdom of Bahrain

Kanoo Performance is a one-stop automotive destination for personalization and customization — the kind of place where a McLaren P1 gets showroom prep next to a numbers-matching 1960 Corvette on the lift, while a Toyota Supra screams through a pull on the in-house dyno and a freshly wrapped McLaren 570S rolls out in gloss Tiffany blue.

Based in Tubli, Manama, the facility is built to handle everything a hyper, luxury, sports or classic car needs — regardless of make — under one roof: body work, tuning, wraps, protection and maintenance.

Full-Service Body Shop

Wide-body kits, panel work, painting & restomod builds — from classics to modern exotics.

In-House Dyno & ECU Tuning

Engine dyno testing and rolling-road tuning on site, with live telemetry readouts.

Official Performance Dealer

Authorized dealer for ARB, IPD Plenums & Xtreme Motorsport, plus Ziebart / LLumar protection.

What We Do

Every stage of a build, in one facility

From a mild refresh to a full track-spec transformation — pick a service below, or configure a complete spec in the builder.

Body Kits & Wide-Body

Front lips, side skirts, flared arches, diffusers & wings — bolt-on or full widebody conversions.

Wraps & Color Change

Full-body gloss, satin & matte color-change wraps in any finish — reversible, custom, camera-ready.

PPF & Ceramic Protection

Ziebart & LLumar paint protection film and ceramic coatings to keep every build showroom-fresh.

ECU Tuning & Dyno

In-house engine dyno and rolling-road tuning, from Stage 1 remaps to full built-engine calibration.

Suspension & Coilovers

Lowering springs, coilovers & air suspension setups, corner-balanced and aligned in-house.

Wheels, Tires & Alignment

Forged & concave wheel fitment, tire selection and precision 4-wheel alignment on the rack.

Performance Exhaust

Cat-back, valvetronic & full titanium systems — tuned for sound, flow and weight savings.

GTR R35 & Engine Rebuilds

Dedicated GT-R specialists, plus full engine rebuild, restoration & classic-car mechanical work.

Window Tint & Detailing

Legal & ceramic IR tint, full interior/exterior detailing to send every build out camera-ready.
