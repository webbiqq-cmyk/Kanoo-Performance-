Who we are

One roof.Every discipline of the build.

Kanoo Performance is a one-stop destination for the serious car owner in Bahrain — the place where a McLaren gets showroom prep on the same floor as a numbers-matching C3 Corvette on the lift, while a 718 Boxster runs a pull on the in-house dyno next door.

Backed by Kanoo Motors and partnered with Garrett Advancing Motion, the workshop pairs factory-grade equipment with fabricators who build one car at a time, to a standard our clients can hand to a concours judge or a track marshal.
